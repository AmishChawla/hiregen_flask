A note on the two spreadsheets

The dashboard and the ROI model contain live formulas, not screenshots of formulas. Paste your ATS export into the Raw sheet and the dashboard calculates. In the ROI model, every blue cell is an input and every assumption states where the number should come from — none of them are benchmarks, because a benchmark from someone else's funnel is the fastest way to have a business case dismissed.
