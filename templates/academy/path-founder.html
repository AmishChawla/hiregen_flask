For founders hiring without a recruiter

12 lessons  ·  About 2 hours

You are hiring your second, fifth or tenth person. You do not have an ATS, a recruiting function, or time to read ten hours of anything.

Skips ATS configuration, analytics and agent design — all of which assume a team and a volume you do not have yet.
