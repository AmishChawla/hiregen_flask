For compliance, legal and HR leadership

14 lessons  ·  About 2.5 hours

You are accountable for AI in hiring without operating it day to day. You need to know what the tools do, what can go wrong, and what to ask for.

Skips the hands-on sourcing and outreach craft, which you are not the one performing.
